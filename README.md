# PokeSurvivor — Play, Partners and Run Plans

PokeSurvivor is a free browser survival game inspired by Pokémon. Choose a partner, move through waves of enemies and collect experience while your selected attack fires automatically. Each level gives you another choice: strengthen a move, learn a different attack or take a stat card that helps your current run.

[PokeSurvivor.me](https://pokesurvivor.me/) is an independent fan guide. It helps you look up a partner, find an evolution requirement or decide what to try next. The game itself is operated separately at [pokesurvivor.com](https://pokesurvivor.com/en/).

## Your first run

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move. Switch active moves with 1–4 or Q/E, and pause with Esc. On a phone or tablet, use landscape mode, drag to move and tap the move you want to select.

Controllers are configured in Settings → Controls. Optional Manual aim in Settings → Accessibility changes aiming direction while attacks still fire automatically. Check whether a damaging move is Physical or Special: your partner uses a different attack factor for each category. Swords Dance and Dragon Dance boost physical attacks; Nasty Plot boosts special attacks.

Keep an escape route open while collecting experience. Focus one attack before spreading upgrades across several moves. A level-5 attack may still need a particular stat card before its gold evolution becomes available: Ember, for example, needs Power to become Fire Blast.

Separate active slots do not all attack together. Use switching for coverage; a Rift Bond can combine learned attacks into one active slot. If your intended upgrade is unavailable, take something that helps you survive the next wave rather than forcing the original plan.

## Common questions

**Is the game free?** Yes. The official game can be played in a browser without downloading a client. This fan guide is free to browse as well.

**Can I save as a guest?** Do not rely on a guest session for saved progress. Use the official game’s account options for cloud saves. This guide cannot access or recover game accounts.

**When does my account upload to the cloud?** Progress is recorded in the current browser first. Automatic uploads are scheduled after about 30 minutes with pending changes. Before leaving or changing devices, press Save at the top right and wait for the pending status to clear. A recent upload may briefly delay another one.

**Can I play with friends?** The game supports co-op for up to four players through its own friends list and room invitations.

**Does pity guarantee the Pokémon I want?** No. It guarantees a rarity. A banner’s counter at 69 means the next pull is guaranteed 5-star; a 5-star result resets that region’s counter. The species still depends on the eligible pool.

**Is this an official website?** No. PokeSurvivor.me is independent of the game operator, Nintendo and The Pokémon Company. Advice helps you understand choices; it does not guarantee a score or a particular outcome.
